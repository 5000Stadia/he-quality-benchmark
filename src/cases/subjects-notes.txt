Interface
SUBJECTS contains labels, actual approximate envelope dimensions in metres, fixed numeric
camera poses/FOVs, seed, description and roles. SUBJECT_ROLES is also exported separately.
createSubject(id, materials, 'master'|'reduced') passes material objects through unchanged.
Roles are preserved as mesh-name prefixes. Batched car/chair meshes retain source part
names in mesh.userData.parts. Tree exposes leafCount=1020 and branchCount=127 (including
trunk and seven root flares) on group.userData. +Y up, front +Z, ground near y=0.
Fixed ground-contact recipe offsets are shared between tiers, avoiding independent
normalization changes; min y remains 0.0001–0.0006m because tessellation samples differ.

Design construction
Car: longitudinal curved body sections use rounded superellipse cross sections with
wheel-arch side cutouts. Separate convex swept glazing, painted roof/pillars, sill trim,
mirrors, modeled door/hood gaps, handles, bumper edges, grille bars, 10-spoke wheels,
40 tread blocks per tire, rounded head/tail lamp housings. Rounded body highlight
continuity and tessellation are meaningful geometry comparison targets. Nominal body
width ~1.85m; mirror envelope 2.15m. Approx. 1.414m high and 4.438m long.
Tree: curved tapered trunk, seven root flares, 17 radial branches, six subordinate twigs
per branch, ten individually positioned curved alpha leaf cards per twig. The seeded
recipe consumes identical randomness in both tiers. Leaf locations and count never
change with tier; curved cards become planar in reduced tier. Two role meshes total.
Chair: rounded seat/back cushions, raked back, timber underframe, four splayed tapered
legs, curved arms and posts. Cushion perimeter piping, individual 6mm stitch dashes at
~14mm pitch, and two tailored seat lines are geometric. Three role meshes total.

UV physical scale
Body/glazing and tube UV distances are authored in metres (body circumference uses a
4.4m nominal unwrap, longitudinal 4.4m). Cabin nominal width unwrap 2.1m, length 2.21m.
Rounded cushions and timber boxes use local metric planar UVs per dominant normal.
Cylinder/torus unwraps use nominal circumference and length in metres; tread uses metric
planar projection. Tube v is curve arc length, u is initial circumference, so taper
retains a stable root-scale unwrap. Materials repeating once per UV unit therefore
repeat nominally once per metre; parent materials may deliberately set their own repeat.
Leaf UVs exclusively normalized [0,1] per card for an alpha silhouette; authored leaf
lengths are 0.15–0.235m. Leaf textures must use the matching silhouette orientation.
All six builds include finite position, normal, UV and tangent buffers. Role batches
merge coincident identical attributes into indexed geometry then calculate tangents.
Projection seams/cylinder caps remain intentionally split where attributes differ.

Limitations and retries
These are procedural study assets, not photorealistic scanned or production-ready assets.
There is no car interior, suspension, transmissive glass setup, tire deformation, rig,
wind deformation, manufactured stitch topology, or watertight production CAD promise.
Glass remains whatever opaque material the parent supplies; expensive transmission is
an independent parent toggle. Light material is shared front/rear; material treatment
is controlled by parent. Rounded box upholstery UVs have projection boundaries; inspect
fabric repeat continuity and grazing views during browser review. Alpha leaf material
must be DoubleSide or use an appropriate double-sided alpha policy.
One local geometry correction reversed custom-surface index winding and cabin sweep
orientation to produce outward normals; one refinement added metric primitive UVs,
indexed batched tangents, mesh-role selection independent of material identity, ground
contact offsets, and actual envelope metadata. No browser aesthetic approval is claimed.
Parent owns integration, captures, shader inspection and final camera/visual validation.

Parent-review refinement: silhouette poses now use near-horizontal +X side/profile
views, distinct from the three-quarter medium views. Parent may fit distances for
viewport aspect and records actual resulting poses. Material tile widths from parent:
paint .25m, bark .45m, fabric .20m, wood .50m; metric UVs support global texture.repeat
values of 4, 2.222..., 5, and 2 respectively. Leaves stay normalized alpha atlas UVs.
