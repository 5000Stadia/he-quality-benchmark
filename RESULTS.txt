Best-supported recommendation: hybrid Three.js pipeline

Use recipes for regular editable furniture and optimized mesh assets for intricate objects. Keep a shared reusable material library; AVIF90 is the strongest small-download close-up candidate in these samples, AVIF80 is a good smaller tier. Run expensive material preparation in workers. GPU-compressed tiers reduce residency, but their quality and payload tradeoffs must be evaluated per material. Keep a high-detail source master and generate delivery tiers offline.

Extend HE's existing instancing, terrain/tree LOD and streaming to the appropriate prop workloads; do not replace it with a universal voxel or splat engine on the evidence here. Prioritize close/interactable objects, select detail by projected error, use transition hysteresis, and enforce decoded CPU/GPU byte budgets plus per-frame upload/build budgets. Admission control must act before resource exhaustion. No finite mobile renderer can retain arbitrarily many unique full-detail objects simultaneously.

Controlled platform: Chromium + actual AMD RX460 GPU, Three.js r160 WebGL. Additional r186.1 WebGL material smoke/performance test passed, but no full WebGPU engine benchmark was run. Extreme-count tests use 585x1140 output, shadows off, 20 size variants of one desk. Material close-ups use 960x720 with shadows on. These are synthetic workloads, not measurements of HE's complete runtime or a real phone.

TIMING AND CONFIDENCE
This expanded experiment began 2026-10-16 22:05:10 UTC. Existing generated references were reused: their original generation calls totaled 46.597 seconds. Encoding, geometry conversion and runtime construction are separately timed above. Fresh concept-to-finished artistic authoring time is NOT established for every method and should not be inferred from codec time. The benchmark scripts and exact encoding commands are retained.

Confident conclusions: share reusable materials; avoid object-specific projected color atlases for general PBR; preserve high-resolution masters; use compact recipes or optimized meshes according to shape; use LOD and bounded residency rather than raw object-count promises. The tested simple voxel conversion is not a better close-up furniture route. AVIF90/80 plus worker processing is the best tested download/close-up balance for these materials, with a substantial uncompressed residency cost.

Remaining validation before production choice: actual target phones and browsers, thermal sustained motion, frame-time tails under real input, diverse material/object catalogs, true streamed camera paths, occlusion in interiors, LOD transition quality, worker-pool concurrency/cancellation, byte-budgeted eviction under network variability, r186 WebGPU versus WebGL, and independently authored PBR height/roughness sources. No claim of universal optimum or guaranteed mobile FPS is made.
