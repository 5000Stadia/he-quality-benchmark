What this study can establish

The subjects and surface channels are original authored test fixtures. Matching views isolate changes to texture delivery or geometry. These are comparisons against an authored reference, not a realism score or a full HE/Photonic engine benchmark.

One person's tie does not establish that everyone will see no difference. Frame intervals reflect this browser and screen refresh rate. Texture and buffer bytes describe payload accounting, not measured driver memory. First rendering includes preparation and compilation.

There is no account, analytics or automatic upload. A hidden tab or graphics-context loss interrupts the current trial. The original desk benchmark and its iPhone 13 evidence remain available.
