IPHONE13 — NINE SUPPLIED BLIND CHOICES
Session 2026-10-16 local / 2026-10-17 UTC; interpretation 2026-10-16.

What these choices support
--------------------------
The observer reported no visible difference for all six texture pairs: authored
lossless reference versus UASTC1024 and UASTC512, on car, tree and chair. The
higher-detail car and chair geometry were preferred; tree geometry was a tie.
For this observer and presentation, reducing texture payload was less visibly
consequential than reducing the curved car/chair geometry. This supports keeping
512 as a serious cheaper candidate while protecting salient nearby geometry;
it does not establish universal equivalence or a production default.

Meaningful surface savings
--------------------------
MiB below means 1,048,576 bytes. Encoded totals include the used material
families' color, normal and roughness files: paint; bark+leaf; fabric+wood.
They exclude shared renderer/recipe/transcoder and geometry. These are asset
file bytes, not measured HTTP transfer with headers/cache/server compression.

subject | encoded MiB reference / UASTC1024 / UASTC512 | mip MiB ref / 1024 / 512
car     | 1.840 / 1.857 / 0.440 | 16 / 4 / 1
tree    | 3.463 / 3.799 / 1.108 | 32 / 8 / 2
chair   | 4.154 / 3.418 / 0.966 | 32 / 8 / 2

UASTC512 saves encoded surface bytes by 76.09% car, 68.00% tree, 76.74% chair,
and mip payload by approximately 93.75% for each. UASTC1024 saves approximately
75% of mip payload, but encoded car/tree files grow 0.92%/9.72%; chair falls
17.71%. Smaller GPU payload and smaller download are separate outcomes.
Rounded mip figures include full chains; exact small-block overhead remains
in the selected JSON. They describe one representation's surface payload,
not measured driver VRAM, total CPU memory or a whole-scene residency budget.
Shared materials across many instances can amortize these costs.

Native reference maps are authored 1024x1024 channels; UASTC1024 changes encoding
of all three channels, and UASTC512 also halves each dimension. These results
compare those delivery pipelines; they do not isolate compression from
resolution loss at 512. No AVIF, ETC1S, alternative model-generation pipeline,
Photonic skin/hair/animated garment or combined reduced-geometry/texture pair
was judged in this nine-pair session.

Presentation and limits
-----------------------
All saved vote snapshots are static (motion=false, timeline=0): car geometry
uses the medium view; the other eight use close. These snapshots do not prove
which earlier views or motion the person may have inspected. No switch-check
records were supplied. Common settings: studio, clean/satin, exposure .95,
MSAA, shadows/wind/refraction off; output 549x620 pixels at capped DPR1.5
(CSS 366x414), despite the phone reporting devicePixelRatio3.

One observer/phone, one saved judgment per pair: ties are observations, not a
population threshold or proof that no detail was lost. The phone UI sometimes
prevented comments or answering; empty notes cannot be interpreted as absence
of defects. Recorded choices are present, but no unrecorded intent is inferred.
An independent UI review also found that Next could leave the new canvas wholly
above the viewport while answer controls remained visible. Whether this affected
any supplied judgment is unknown. Preserve all nine first choices, treat ties as
provisional, and do not claim verified viewing of both candidates or motion.
Repair build 7396763086827e49 brings each new pair canvas into view. Five
automated desktop touch journeys passed; native Safari has not been retested.
Three interruptions are separately recorded, including a hidden-tab event;
none is counted as an additional vote. The final current trial is stopped.

There are no steady measurements, FPS or GPU timestamp samples in this supplied
result. Asynchronous GPU timing is reported unavailable. First useful subject

What to test next
-----------------
Keep these completed nine answers as evidence; repeating them merely to obtain
the same static ties is not the next discriminating question. Inspect moving
leaf edges/twigs, paint highlights and cloth seams at matched camera/light
phases, especially UASTC512 and reduced tree geometry, and capture defect notes
once comment entry is reliable. Protect the car/chair curves already preferred.
Then measure the chosen candidates in a mixed scene with actual HE renderer
paths and resource admission. HE already streams terrain/tree tiers, and

cannot select animated-person tiers or reserve their runtime headroom.
